Data Deletion Request

At HerCycle Bloom, we respect your privacy and your right to control your personal data. You have the right to permanently delete your account and all associated health data at any time.

Option 1: Delete via the App (Recommended)

You can instantly and permanently delete your data directly within the HerCycle Bloom app:

  1. Open the HerCycle Bloom app and log in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  4. Follow the on-screen prompts to type "DELETE" and confirm.

Note: This action is immediate and cannot be undone. All cycle data, body metrics, and profile info will be wiped from our servers.

Option 2: Delete via Email Request

If you no longer have access to the app, you can request account deletion by emailing our support team from the email address associated with your HerCycle Bloom account.

Please include "Account Deletion Request" in the subject line.

Email Support to Delete Account

We will process email requests within 5 business days.
