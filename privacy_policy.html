HerCycle Bloom Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Welcome to HerCycle Bloom ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal and health data when you use the HerCycle Bloom application. By using HerCycle Bloom, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Data We Collect

We collect the following information that you provide directly to the application:

  • Account Data: Email address and display name provided during sign-up.
  • Health & Wellness Data: Cycle dates, symptom logs, body metrics (age, height, weight, BMI), pregnancy milestones, and lifestyle choices.
  • Technical Data: Anonymized app performance and crash reports used solely to improve application reliability.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used strictly to provide and enhance your HerCycle Bloom experience, including:

  • Calculating cycle phase predictions, fertility window estimates, and wellness insights.
  • Providing personalized wellness guidance via HerCycle Bloom AI.
  • Storing your preferences securely and synchronizing your account.

3. Data Protection & Privacy

Your health and personal data belong entirely to you. HerCycle Bloom is built with privacy-first principles:

  • We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your health or personal data to third parties or advertisers.
  • Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest using industry-standard security protocols (Google Firebase).

4. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your data only for as long as your account remains active. You can request permanent deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time directly in the app via Profile > Delete Account, or by emailing us at sajawal0005@gmail.com.

5. Account Deletion

HerCycle Bloom gives you full control over your data. You may permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time directly within the app. This section explains exactly how the account deletion process works.

5.1 How to Delete Your Account (In-App Steps)

Follow these exact steps to permanently delete your account:

  1. Open the HerCycle Bloom app and sign in.
  2. Tap the Profile tab at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down to the Data Management section.
  4. Tap Delete Account (identified by a red delete icon).
  5. You will be taken to the Delete Account screen. A warning banner will appear stating that this action is permanent.
  6. You must type the word "DELETE" (capital letters) into the confirmation text field.
  7. Once typed correctly, tap the "Delete My Account" button to confirm.

As an additional email-based option, you may also request account deletion by sending an email to sajawal0005@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account.

5.2 What Data Is Deleted

Upon confirming account deletion, the following actions occur automatically:

All locally stored data is permanently erased from your device via the Isar database:

  • All cycle and menstruation logs
  • Symptom, mood, and note history
  • Body metrics and BMI records
  • Kick logs and pregnancy appointment records
  • Pregnancy journey tracking data
  • All app notifications
  • AI coach conversations (Premium users)
  • Account profile information and app preferences

Your Firebase Authentication account is permanently deleted (user.delete()), and you are automatically signed out and redirected to the login screen.

5.3 What Data Is Retained

After account deletion, the following data may still exist on our servers:

  • Anonymized, non-personally-identifiable analytics and crash reports used solely to improve app reliability.
  • Server-side audit logs generated by the billing verification system (Google Play purchase records processed via Firebase Cloud Functions), which are retained only as required by legal and financial compliance obligations.

No personally identifiable health, cycle, or profile data linked to your account remains retrievable after deletion.

5.4 Data Retention Period

All personal data is retained only for as long as your account remains active. Once you delete your account via the in-app flow described above, all associated personal data is immediately and permanently purged. No personal data is retained beyond what is legally required for financial/billing audit purposes.

5.5 Important Notice

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. There is no way to recover your data once deleted. If you signed in using Google, you may need to sign out and back in if the deletion does not complete immediately. If you only wish to remove your data while keeping your account, use the "Start Fresh" option in Profile > Data Management, which clears all local cycle and body metrics data but does not delete your Firebase account.
